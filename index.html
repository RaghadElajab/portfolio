AI · COMPUTER VISION

Face Recognition Attendance

A live attendance platform that registers students, identifies multiple faces and saves session reports.

PythonFaceNetSVMOpenCVFastAPI
  • Recognizes multiple students live and rejects unknown faces.
  • Connects registration, attendance and session recordings.
  • Registration: A mobile-friendly FastAPI portal captures or uploads student photos.
  • Recognition: Aligned faces become 512-dimensional FaceNet embeddings, classified by an RBF SVM.

04

AI · DEEP LEARNING

Crop Disease Classification

A rigorous PyTorch comparison of four CNN architectures across 12 maize and cashew health conditions.

PythonPyTorchEfficientNet
  • Compared four CNNs across 11,838 images and 12 crop conditions.
  • EfficientNet-B0 achieved 95.73% cashew test accuracy.
  • Dataset: 11,838 images across five cashew and seven maize conditions.
  • Training: Stratified splits, augmentation, ImageNet normalization and class-weighted loss.
  • Models: A custom CNN, ResNet-18, EfficientNet-B0 and VGG-16, with early stopping and best-checkpoint restoration.
  • Evaluation: Held-out accuracy, precision, recall, F1, confusion matrices and ROC curves; EfficientNet-B0 reached 85.89% on maize.

SYSTEMS · NETWORKING

Networked CLI Shell

A concurrent remote Unix shell built in C around TCP sockets and POSIX process control.

CPOSIXTCP Socketspthreads
  • Runs remote commands with isolated client sessions.
  • Supports Unix pipelines of up to four processes.
  • Client/server: An interactive localhost client sends commands and receives output over TCP.
  • Execution: Allowlisted commands use fork and exec; dup2 redirects output to the socket.
  • Pipelines: Up to four commands run through Unix pipes, with wait coordinating process cleanup.
  • Sessions: Each client keeps its own working-directory path, with POSIX threads and FIFO scheduling.
